Privacy Policy

Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer · Last updated 14 November 2026

Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer is an educational game for children aged 7 to 12, made by the Binary Bandits team for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game keeps on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves these on your own device only:

  • the astronaut name you type or pick,
  • your chosen job, level and settings (read-aloud, reduce motion),
  • your training progress: rank, badges, drill scores and a short mission log.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or your browser's site data.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, tracking or location data.
  • No chat, and no way to contact other players.
  • No links to outside websites, apart from links to NASA's public records of real storms in the mission report.

Read-aloud

If you switch on "Read to me", the game uses a text-to-speech voice built into your device. The game only uses voices that work on the device, so the words are not sent to any online service.

NASA data

The real solar storm records in the game come from NASA's DONKI space weather database. They are built into the game, so playing does not contact NASA or anyone else.

Children

The game is made for children. Because we collect no personal information, we have nothing to share, sell or disclose. Parents and teachers are welcome to contact us with any questions.
